Grammar Matrix: Starter to Unit 3

Review the rules side by side before starting the practice challenges.

1. Articles (A vs An)

• a + consonant sound: a pen, a book, a ruler
• an + vowel sound: an apple, an eraser, an umbrella

2. My / Your & Questions

• my = belongs to me: my kite, my doll
• your = belongs to you: your train
• Is this...? → Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

3. This is vs These are

• This is (1 item): This is my nose.
• These are (2+ items): These are my legs.
• Plural: add -s (ear → ears)

Teacher Tip: Check question words (What for things, Who for people, How old for age) and correct usage of a/an.

Roleplay: Meeting at School

Jamie: Hello! What's your name?

Rosy: Hi! My name's Rosy. How old are you, Jamie?

Jamie: I'm seven. Look, what's this in my school bag?

Rosy: It's an apple and a new ruler!

Jamie: Yes, it is! And is this your toy car on the desk?

Rosy: No, it isn't. That is Tim's car. This is my teddy!

Jamie: Oh, it's cute! Look at its face. The eyes are brown and the ears are small!

Rosy: Yes, and these are its fluffy arms!
