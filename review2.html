Grammar Matrix: Units 4 to 6

Review the three core pillars side by side before starting the practice rounds.

1. Jobs & He / She

• He's a / She's an...
• Is he/she a...?
→ Yes, he/she is.
→ No, he/she isn't.

2. Where's & In / On / Under

• Where's the...?
• in: in the pool / net
• on: on the slide / swing
• under: under the tree

3. I'm, You're & 's

• I'm / You're
• Are you...?
→ Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
• 's: Tim's dad, Billy's toy

Teacher Tip: Watch for pronoun subject alignment: Is the question asking about he, she, or you? Ensure students do not mix up "Yes, he is" with "Yes, I am".

Roleplay: Family Day at the Park

Tim: Hi Rosy! Are you at the park with your family today?

Rosy: Yes, I am! Look, that man under the big tree is my dad.

Tim: Oh! Is he a firefighter?

Rosy: No, he isn't. He's a pilot! And that lady on the bench is my aunt.

Tim: She's a doctor, right?

Rosy: Yes, she is! Now, where's Billy? I have his red frisbee.

Tim: Look over there! He's on the slide with Jamie.

Rosy: Great! Let's put Billy's frisbee on the grass and play together!
