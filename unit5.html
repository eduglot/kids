Grammar Rules: Where's...? & Prepositions of Place

1. Asking Where:
• Where's = Where is (asking for the position or location of an item/person).
• Examples: Where's the ball? Where's Billy? Where's the kite?


2. Prepositions of Place:
• in (inside something): It's in the pool. It's in the net. It's in the bag.
• on (touching the top surface): It's on the slide. It's on the seesaw. It's on the swing.
• under (below or beneath): It's under the tree. He's under the seesaw.

📥 IN (Inside)

• in the pool
• in the net
• in the box
🔝 ON (On top of)

• on the slide
• on the swing
• on the seesaw
🔽 UNDER (Beneath)

• under the tree
• under the swing
• under the table

Part B: Where's Questions & Location Sentences (8 Questions)

1. [ the ball / Where's / ? ]
2. ⚽ Where's the ball? (on / slide) →
3. [ kite / the / Where's / ? ]
4. 🪁 Where's the kite? (in / tree) →
5. [ Billy / Where's / ? ]
6. 👦 Where's Billy? (under / seesaw) →
7. [ the frisbee / Where's / ? ]
8. 🥏 Where's the frisbee? (under / swing) →
Teacher Tip: Pair roleplay! Student A plays Tim searching for his lost toys, and Student B plays Alison giving park directions.

Conversation Practice

Tim: Oh no! Where's my new red frisbee, Alison?

Alison: Look over there! It's under the swing!

Tim: Whew, thank you! And where's my green soccer ball?

Alison: It's in the goal net. See?

Tim: Got it! Now, where's Billy? I can't see him.

Alison: Haha! Look at the playground. He's on the slide!

Tim: Awesome! Let's go play on the seesaw with him!

Teacher Tip: Interactive Hide-and-Seek! A student places an eraser, pen, or toy somewhere on their desk (in a mug, on a book, under a keyboard) and asks: "Where's my...?" Their partner guesses the location.
