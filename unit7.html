🎯 Lesson Objectives

  • Identify 8 core items of clothing: dress, socks, T-shirt, trousers, shorts, shoes, coat, and hat.
  • Accurately apply 3rd person possessive adjectives: his (boys) and her (girls).
  • Form and answer plural Yes/No questions with 'be': "Are they his / her...?" → "Yes, they are. / No, they aren't."
  • Distinguish singular (*This is his T-shirt*) from plural clothes (*These are her socks*).

Grammar Rules: His / Her & Are they...?

1. Possessive Adjectives:
• his = belongs to a boy/man: his T-shirt, his shoes, his coat
• her = belongs to a girl/woman: her dress, her socks, her hat


2. Plural Clothes (always with -s):
• trousers, shorts, socks, shoes are plural → use they / are!
• Statement: They're his shorts. (= They are his shorts.)


3. Yes/No Questions (Are they...?):
• Question: Are they her shoes? / Are they his trousers?
• Positive: Yes, they are.
• Negative: No, they aren't. (aren't = are not)

👦 HIS (Boy / Man)

• This is his T-shirt.
• These are his shoes.
• This is his coat.
👧 HER (Girl / Woman)

• This is her dress.
• These are her socks.
• This is her hat.
❓ ARE THEY...?

• Are they his shorts?
→ Yes, they are.
→ No, they aren't.

Engoo-Style Conversation Practice: Sorting the Laundry

Rosy: Look at the clean clothes on the basket, Tim! Can you help me sort them?

Tim: Sure! Is this your dress?

Rosy: Yes, it is. That's my pink dress. And what about this green T-shirt?

Tim: That is Jamie's. It's his T-shirt!

Rosy: Okay! Are they his shorts?

Tim: Yes, they are! They're his blue shorts.

Rosy: And are they his socks?

Tim: No, they aren't! Look at the flowers. They're Alison's socks!
