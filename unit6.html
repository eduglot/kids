🎯 Lesson Objectives

  • Identify 8 core family members: mum, dad, brother, sister, grandma, grandpa, aunt, and uncle.
  • Apply first and second person subject pronouns with contractions: I'm (I am) and you're (you are).
  • Ask and answer yes/no questions with 'be': "Are you...?" → "Yes, I am. / No, I'm not."
  • Express ownership and family relationships using possessive 's (e.g., Rosy's mum, Tim's ball).

Grammar Rules: Pronouns (I / You) & Possessive 's

1. Pronouns (I'm / You're):
• I'm = I am: I'm a pupil. I'm seven.
• You're = You are: You're my teacher. You're my friend.


2. Question & Short Answers (Are you...?):
• Question: Are you happy? Are you Jamie's sister?
• Positive: Yes, I am.
• Negative: No, I'm not. (Never say "No, I amn't")


3. Possessive 's (Belonging & Relationship):
• Person + 's + Noun: Rosy's mum, Tim's sister, Billy's teddy

🙋 I'M (I am)

• I'm a student.
• I'm happy.
• I'm six years old.
👉 YOU'RE (You are)

• You're my friend.
• You're a good pupil.
• You're seven.
🔗 POSSESSIVE 'S

• Rosy's dad
• Tim's kite
• Jamie's brother
Teacher Tip: Focus on whether students can differentiate between the pronoun contractions (I'm / You're) and the possessive apostrophe 's.

Conversation Practice

Jamie: Hello! Look at my family photo album, Rosy.

Rosy: Oh wonderful! Are you seven in this picture?

Jamie: Yes, I am! And look, this is my dad and this is my mum.

Rosy: Is this your brother Billy?

Jamie: Yes, it is! That is Billy's favorite teddy bear.

Rosy: And who is this smiling lady with the cookies?

Jamie: That is my grandma! She's my mum's mum.

Rosy: You're lucky! You have a wonderful family, Jamie.
