🎯 Lesson Objectives

  • Identify parts of a house: bedroom, bathroom, living room, kitchen, dining room, and garden.
  • Learn adverbs of location: upstairs and downstairs.
  • Ask where people are using plural questions: "Where are...?"
  • Ask and answer Yes/No questions about rooms: "Is he in the...? Are they in the...?"
Teacher Tip: Warm-up! Ask students: "Where do you sleep? Where do you eat?" Click the cards below to drill the pronunciation. Make sure to clearly explain the difference between 'upstairs' (upper floors) and 'downstairs' (lower floors).

Grammar Rules: Where are & In the...

1. Asking Where (Plural):
• Where are...? (Asking about 2 or more people/things).
• Examples: Where are Mum and Dad? Where are they?


2. Upstairs & Downstairs:
• Adverbs of place do not need "in". Just say: He's upstairs. They're downstairs.


3. Yes/No Questions (Is he/she...? Are they...?):
• Is he in the bedroom? → Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
• Is she in the kitchen? → Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
• Are they in the living room? → Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

❓ WHERE ARE...?

• Where are they?
• They're in the garden.
• They're downstairs.
🪜 UPSTAIRS / DOWNSTAIRS

• She is upstairs.
• He is downstairs.
(No "in" needed!)
✅ YES/NO QUESTIONS

• Is he in the kitchen?
• Are they in the garden?
→ Yes, they are.
Teacher Tip: Ensure students do not say "in the upstairs" or "in downstairs". Remind them that upstairs/downstairs act alone.

Part A: Where are they? (8 Questions)

1. Billy? (1 person)
2. Mum and Dad? (2 people)
3. Billy is bedroom. 🛏️
4. My bedroom is . ⬆️
5. the girls?
6. They are . ⬇️
7. We eat dinner dining room. 🍽️
8. I watch TV in the living room. 🛋️
Teacher Tip: Watch for subject agreement. If the question is "Is he...", the answer must use "he". If the question is "Are they...", the answer must use "they".

Part B: Yes/No Questions in the Home (8 Questions)

1. [ he / Is / the kitchen / in / ? ]
2. 🍳 Is he in the kitchen? (Yes) →
3. [ the bedroom / she / in / Is / ? ]
4. 🛏️ Is she in the bedroom? (No) →
5. [ they / in / the garden / Are / ? ]
6. 🌷 Are they in the garden? (Yes) →
7. [ the living room / Are / in / they / ? ]
8. 🛋️ Are they in the living room? (No) →
Teacher Tip: House tour roleplay! Have Student A read Tim and Student B read Rosy. Remind them to use expressive voices as they pretend to look around the house.

Engoo-Style Conversation Practice: Hide and Seek

Tim: Let's play hide and seek! Where's Billy?

Rosy: Hmm. Is he upstairs in his bedroom?

Tim: Let me check... No, he isn't. He's not upstairs.

Rosy: Look downstairs! Is he in the kitchen with Mum?

Tim: No, he isn't in the kitchen. Where are they?

Rosy: Look outside! Are they in the garden?

Tim: Yes, they are! Billy is playing with his toys in the garden.

Rosy: Found them! Let's go play.

Teacher Tip: "Imaginary House Map" game! Student A thinks of a family member hiding in a specific room. Student B has 3 guesses using "Is he/she in the...?" or "Are they in the...?" to find them.
