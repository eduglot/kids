Grammar Rules: Indefinite Articles

Question: What's this? (= What is this?)

Answer: It's a ... / It's an ...

📘 A + Consonants
b, c, d, p, r, t, w...

• a pencil
• a book
• a desk
• a door
🍎 AN + Vowels
a, e, i, o, u

• an apple
• an eraser
• an orange
• an umbrella
Teacher Tip: Have students take turns reading out the full answer, e.g., "It's an umbrella!"

Part B: Sentence Building (8 Questions)

1. [ this / What's / ? ]
2. [ door / a / It's / . ]
3. [ an / apple / It's / . ]
4. [ ruler / It's / a / . ]
5. [ eraser / It's / an / . ]
6. [ window / a / It's / . ]
7. [ orange / an / It's / . ]
8. [ bag / It's / a / . ]
Teacher Tip: Pair students up. Have them switch roles halfway through the dialogue.

Engoo-Style Conversation Practice

Jamie: Look at my new school backpack, Rosy!

Rosy: Wow! What's this? (Points to a long yellow tool)

Jamie: It's a pencil.

Rosy: And what's this? (Points to a fresh fruit)

Jamie: It's an apple for my lunch!

Rosy: Is that an eraser in the front pocket?

Jamie: Yes, it's an eraser and a ruler!
