Grammar Rules: Ownership & Yes/No Questions

1. Possessive Adjectives:
• I → my (belongs to me): This is my ball. This is my doll.
• You → your (belongs to you): This is your teddy. This is your car.


2. Question & Short Answers:
• Statement: This is your teddy. → Question: Is this your teddy?
• Positive: Yes, it is.
• Negative: No, it isn't. (isn't = is not)

🙋 MY (Belongs to Me)

• This is my train.
• This is my puzzle.
• This is my bike.
👉 YOUR (Belongs to You)

• This is your teddy.
• This is your doll.
• This is your car.
Teacher Tip: Have students say the complete sentence aloud once they select the possessive adjective.

Engoo-Style Conversation Practice

Tim: Hi Jamie! Is this your toy box?

Jamie: Yes, it is! Come and look at my toys.

Tim: Cool! Is this your red car?

Jamie: No, it isn't. That is my brother Billy's car. This blue train is my toy!

Tim: Wow! And is this your green kite?

Jamie: Yes, it is! Let's go fly it in the garden!
