Grammar Rules: Plurals (-s) & Demonstratives (This / These)

1. Singular vs. Plural:
• One thing (Singular): one finger, one eye, one arm
• Two or more (Plural = add -s): three fingers, two eyes, two arms


2. This is vs. These are:
• Singular: This is my nose. / This is my pencil case.
• Plural: These are my eyes. / These are my pencils.


3. Using 'is' and 'are' for identification:
• Singular: The nose is pink.
• Plural: The eyes are blue. The legs are green.

☝️ THIS IS (Singular = 1)

• This is my nose.
• This is my face.
• This is my ball.
✌️ THESE ARE (Plural = 2+)

• These are my ears.
• These are my arms.
• These are my fingers.
Teacher Tip: Focus on whether students include or omit the ending '-s' sound based on the number given.

Conversation Practice

Alison: Look at my drawing, Jamie! It is a funny alien monster!

Jamie: Wow, that looks cool! How many eyes does it have?

Alison: These are the eyes! It has four big green eyes!

Jamie: Haha! And what is this in the middle?

Alison: This is its red nose. And these are three purple arms!

Jamie: Three arms? That is awesome! The legs are yellow too!

Teacher Tip: "Draw My Monster" Activity! Give students 60 seconds on a scrap piece of paper or whiteboard to draw a quick alien with unusual numbers of body parts. Then they describe it on camera using "This is" and "These are".
