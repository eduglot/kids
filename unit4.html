🎯 Lesson Objectives

  • Identify 8 community professions: teacher, pupil, doctor, nurse, firefighter, pilot, police officer, and farmer.
  • Accurately apply third-person singular pronouns: he (boy/man) and she (girl/woman).
  • Combine pronouns with articles: He's a / She's an...
  • Ask and answer yes/no questions: "Is he / she a...?" → "Yes, he/she is. / No, he/she isn't."

Grammar Rules: Pronouns (He / She) & Questions with Is

1. Pronouns & Sentences:
• He is for a boy or man: He's a doctor. (= He is a doctor.)
• She is for a girl or woman: She's a nurse. (= She is a nurse.)
• Always use a / an before singular jobs: He's an astronaut. She's a teacher.


2. Question & Short Answers:
• Statement: She's a teacher. → Question: Is she a teacher?
• Statement: He's a pilot. → Question: Is he a pilot?
• Positive: Yes, he is. / Yes, she is.
• Negative: No, he isn't. / No, she isn't.

Conversation Practice

Tim: Look at this photo, Rosy! Who is this man?

Rosy: That is my uncle David. Can you guess his job?

Tim: Hmm... Is he a firefighter?

Rosy: No, he isn't! Look at his tall hat and airplane badge.

Tim: Oh! Is he a pilot?

Rosy: Yes, he is! He flies big planes. And look at this woman here.

Tim: Is she a doctor?

Rosy: No, she isn't. She's a nurse! She works at the hospital.
